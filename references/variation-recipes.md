# Variation recipes

Choose one composition family and one ink family. Select the actual paper with `background-contrast.md`; the paper suggestions below are fallbacks, not fixed pairings. Avoid repeating the most recent visible recipe in a batch.

## Composition families

1. **Quiet companion** — small seated or standing dog in the lower third; expansive paper above; one environmental cue.
2. **Curled island** — sleeping dog forms one compact oval; surrounding marks dissolve outward; best for warmth and rest.
3. **Profile passage** — side-profile dog walking through two or three compressed landscape bands; calm directional rhythm.
4. **Portrait fragment** — head and upper chest cropped boldly off-center; strongest identity preservation; microtype balances the opposite side.
5. **Doorway pause** — dog framed by an implied threshold made from two incomplete color blocks; no literal decorative border.
6. **Weather field** — small dog anchored inside a larger field of rain, snow, wind, grass, or light rendered as sparse carved marks.
7. **Paired orbit** — two dogs with one visible relationship and generous space between or around them; avoid symmetry and sticker layout.
8. **Type and dog** — one small carved dog plus one short title used as a structural counterweight; image remains primary.
9. **Fused companion** — complete dog small in the lower third and slightly left of center; one faint structural echo at the far upper-left; one low-contrast object whisper at the lower-right; center and upper-right remain blank. Use the exact measurements in `reference-fusion.md`.

## Abstraction levels

- **A1 Recognizable portrait:** preserve anatomy, markings, pose, and expression; simplify environment.
- **A2 Interpreted dog:** preserve silhouette and three to six anchors; rebuild 30%–50% of the scene as carved planes and exposed-paper gaps. Default.
- **A3 Poetic trace:** retain two or three dog cues and prioritize mood. Use only when identity is not critical.

## Palette families

- **Moss + persimmon:** deep forest green and warm orange; prefer pale mineral blue or cool gray paper for warm coats.
- **Mineral rain:** cobalt/mineral blue, blue-gray, tiny vermilion; prefer oat or pale peach-gray paper for cool coats.
- **Apricot dusk:** aubergine, apricot, dusty coral; prefer misty blue-gray or desaturated lavender paper for apricot/brown coats.
- **Clear morning:** fresh green, sky cyan, lemon; prefer muted warm-gray paper unless the coat is warm yellow or brown.
- **Quiet night:** violet-blue, muted teal, small amber; prefer pale oat or soft peach-gray paper for blue-gray coats.
- **Rust studio:** rust red, slate blue, muted pink; prefer cool sage-gray or mineral blue-gray paper for rust/brown coats.

## Batch discipline

For a set, keep paper behavior, carved-line grammar, and typography scale fixed. Change at least composition family, pose, abstraction structure, and accent placement between adjacent images. Never create a batch by moving the same dog cutout around the page.
