# Dog Woodcut Zine

Dog Woodcut Zine 是一个面向 Codex 的图像生成 Skill，用于把宠物照片、品种描述或简短情境转化为安静、轻盈的当代木刻 zine 插画。

它不仅应用木刻纹理，还会保留宠物的脸部结构、毛发轮廓、毛色分布、神态、体型关系与重要配饰。Skill 会根据宠物毛色自动选择具有足够对比度的纸张背景，并从原照片中提取少量环境元素，在保留充分留白的同时增加画面的场景感。

主要能力：

- 根据照片保留单只或多只宠物的身份特征
- 根据主体毛色动态选择纸张颜色
- 从照片中提取帐篷、坐垫、地砖、玩具等环境线索
- 控制木刻线条、墨色、留白和主体比例
- 将小狗绘制风格与背景布局风格融合到一张成品中
- 避免生成拼贴、分屏或相互独立的多张图片
- 检查宠物身份、肢体结构、主体对比度与环境层级

---

Dog Woodcut Zine is a Codex image-generation skill that transforms pet photos, breed descriptions, or short narrative prompts into calm, airy contemporary woodcut-zine illustrations.

Rather than applying a simple texture filter, it preserves recognizable traits such as facial geometry, fur silhouette, coat markings, expression, relative body size, and important accessories. It also selects the paper color according to the pet’s dominant coat and extracts a small number of environmental cues from the source photo while maintaining generous negative space.

Key capabilities:

- Preserve the identity of one or multiple pets from reference photos
- Adapt the paper color to the subject’s coat color and value
- Extract restrained environmental cues such as tents, cushions, floor seams, or toys
- Control carved-line density, ink palette, negative space, and subject scale
- Fuse dog-rendering references and background-system references into one finished canvas
- Prevent split panels, collages, and separately generated components
- Validate pet identity, anatomy, contrast, composition, and environmental hierarchy
